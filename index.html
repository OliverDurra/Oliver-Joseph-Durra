About

Builder, writer, keeper of history

Oliver Joseph Durra is an American CEO, inventor, author and entrepreneur based in Santa Rosa, California. He leads Aeolian Industries, a technology company working at the meeting point of symbolic computation, cybersecurity, quantum systems and ethical artificial intelligence.

He studied Geography at San José State University, earning his B.A. in 2016 — a discipline of maps, places and the long movement of peoples that runs through both his engineering and his writing.

His books bring together theology, history and cosmology. He writes from a Christian conviction that history has a shape and an Author, and that the resurrection of Jesus Christ is the hinge on which time itself turns.

Company

Aeolian Industries

Aeolian Industries is a California company based in Santa Rosa, officially filed with the California Secretary of State on October 27, 2025. Oliver serves as CEO and co-founder alongside Daniel Antonio.

The company describes itself as a next-generation technology consortium. Its foundation is H-Code, a proprietary cryptographic and symbolic architecture on which its security products are built.

Heritage

A line of five thousand years

The Durra family is among the oldest European families in North America. Its genealogy, as Oliver has traced it, reaches back more than 5,000 years — through the Old World and into the founding generations of the New.

That inheritance is the root of his work as a historian. His writing treats lineage not as a list of names but as testimony: families carried faith, learning and memory across exile and migration, and those stories are worth keeping.
